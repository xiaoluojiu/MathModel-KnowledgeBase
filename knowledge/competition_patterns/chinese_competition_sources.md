# Chinese Competition Source Index

## National Undergraduate Mathematical Contest in Modeling

The Chinese mathematical modeling community maintains an archive of excellent-paper collections. A current public index lists the National Undergraduate Mathematical Contest in Modeling excellent papers for 2010-2025, as well as graduate modeling papers and MCM/ICM collections.

Use these collections as a **case-mining source** rather than as an unstructured paper dump.

Recommended extraction fields:

- contest/year/problem
- subproblem decomposition
- problem pattern
- data pattern
- model family
- model role
- preprocessing
- parameter-estimation method
- validation/evaluation
- sensitivity/robustness analysis
- model combination
- innovation claimed by the paper
- evidence strength
- source URL

Source index: https://www.cmathc.org.cn/mcm/lw/

## Important distinction

An award or excellent-paper label is evidence that the work was recognized in that competition context. It is **not evidence that every model used in that paper is universally suitable**. The knowledge base should preserve this distinction during AI retrieval.
